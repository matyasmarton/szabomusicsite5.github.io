Our Roster

Duos

Chemistry, contrast, and conversation in music. Our duos create an intimate dynamic perfect for elegant dining and cocktail settings.

Bellini & Moreau

Violin & Piano Duo

Classical and contemporary repertoire for formal dining and gala evenings. Their playing is marked by an effortless musical conversation that feels both spontaneous and perfectly rehearsed.

The Rivera Duo

Vocal & Guitar

Warm Latin-influenced sound blending original songs with timeless covers. The Riveras bring an infectious energy to poolside evenings and themed cultural nights.

Chen & Hartmann

Harp & Flute Duo

Celestial textures ideal for wellness decks, spa lounges and high tea. Together they create a sound world of extraordinary delicacy and refinement.

Duo Azzurro

Cello & Piano

Italian-trained ensemble with a profound command of the Romantic repertoire. Their performances have a depth and passion that leaves audiences genuinely moved.

Koval & Park

Jazz Vocals & Upright Bass

Stripped-back jazz at its most sophisticated — voice and bass in perfect dialogue. This rare formation creates an intimacy that pulls every listener into its orbit.

Nordvik & Salas

Saxophone & Piano

Contemporary jazz with a Nordic cool — understated elegance for any venue. Their sound is refined, airy and perfectly suited to the luxury cruise atmosphere.

Tanaka & Osei

Koto & Percussion

A fusion of Japanese and West African musical traditions — uniquely captivating and ideal for cultural evenings and themed voyages through Asia or Africa.

Duo Serenade

Soprano & Piano

Art song, opera arias and musical theatre for refined evening programmes. An elegant duo whose versatility spans five centuries of vocal repertoire with authority.

Fitzroy & Lemos

Acoustic Guitar Duo

Two virtuosic guitars weaving intricate arrangements from classical to pop. Their interplay is characterised by a remarkable chemistry that develops with each performance.

Rosenberg & Mwangi

Violin & Marimba

An unexpected pairing that produces a vibrant, cross-cultural soundscape. The contrast between these two timbres creates something genuinely unlike anything else on the circuit.

Duo Lumière

Clarinet & Piano

Parisian sensibility meets concert precision. Their programme blends French impressionism with contemporary works and beloved classical pieces.

Almeida & Luo

Voice & Cajón

Raw, rhythmic and soulful — an intimate act with incredible stage presence. This stripped-down format puts the voice front and centre, supported by the heartbeat of the cajón.

Varga & Steinberg

Oboe & Harpsichord

Baroque specialists recreating the golden age of chamber music with authenticity and passion. Perfect for historical themed voyages and formal heritage dinners.

Duo Constellation

Trumpet & Piano

Bright, bold and celebratory — perfect for festive evenings and themed nights. Their programme encompasses jazz standards, classical gems and rousing crowd favourites.

Kwon & Petrov

Piano Four Hands

The rare spectacle of two pianists sharing one instrument in perfect synchrony. A performance that is as visually compelling as it is musically extraordinary.
